1 · Image Clarity (Multi-class Quality Grade)

Every captured frame is graded 1–4 by the quality classifier before any downstream analysis. Grade 1 images are too poor to analyse and are excluded from ECD screening; grades 2–4 proceed to endothelial cell density estimation.

2 · ECD Screening

Endothelial cell density (ECD) is estimated only for images that pass the clarity gate (grade ≥ 2). Each estimate is compared against a clinical threshold to flag corneas at risk of endothelial insufficiency.
